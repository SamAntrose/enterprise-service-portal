SECTION 2

Project Overview

What is this project?
The Enterprise IT & Corporate Facilities Service Portal is an AI-powered web application that serves as a unified service desk for managing IT support requests, facility maintenance, and emergency operations across an enterprise organization.

Problem Statement:

  • Employees waste time navigating multiple systems to report IT issues or facility problems
  • Technicians lack a filtered, prioritized work queue with SLA visibility
  • Admins have no real-time visibility into ticket volumes and vendor performance
  • Emergency incidents require manual phone calls and emails with no automation

Our Solution: A single-portal platform where employees submit issues in plain English, AI automatically classifies and routes tickets, technicians receive domain-filtered queues with live SLA timers, and emergency SOS triggers real phone calls with automated admin alerts.
